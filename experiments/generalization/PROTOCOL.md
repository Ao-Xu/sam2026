# General-kernel and harmonic-structure experiments

Protocol fixed before outcome inspection. Run `python -X utf8 run.py`, then `python -X utf8 analyze.py`. BLAS threads are fixed to one. All fits independently minimize the samplewise shifted logistic objective plus external ridge; no fitted curve is synthesized from a response formula.

E1 approximates population integrals on a periodic uniform grid, weighted by density (1+a cos x)/(2 pi), a=0,.3,.6. The conditional label mean is epsilon(cos x+b cos2x), epsilon=.15, b=0,.25,.5, hence probabilities are valid. Full factorial grids 256/512, tau=.02/.08, radii0/.005/.02/.1/.25/.5/1 give252 fits. The kernel is the existing closed-form normalized periodic Sobolev kernel (infinite rank). Report signed cosine coefficients with respect to uniform Lebesgue measure, not density-weighted projections. A frequency absent from the label function may arise through the nonuniform input operator already at linear order; therefore label-absent frequencies are not automatically called nonlinear harmonics. Store the independently computed linear-response coefficient as a diagnostic. Grid differences and retained-coordinate stationarity are reported.

E2 uses independent inputs on [-1,1]^2 with product density (1+.6 x1)(1+.4 x2)/4 and normalized Gaussian kernel exp(-||x-z||^2/(2*.7^2)). Balanced labels are independent Rademacher variables. n=128/256/512,100 independent replicates, tau=.02/.08, paired radii0/.5/1 give1800 independently optimized fits. Seed = 1930000 +1000*n+rep. Numerical features factor the training Gram matrix, discarding eigenvalues <=max(1e-13 lambda_max,1e-13); discarded-gradient bound is recorded. Fixed population projections are <f,1>,<f,x1>,<f,x2> in L2(P_X); no sample eigenvectors are compared. Centered covariance of sqrt(n) times these three projections is estimated across replicates. Radius ratios use paired bootstrap across replicate identities (2000 draws); intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty, not uncertainty in the population reference. Test log loss against balanced labels is also recorded as a finite-sample descriptive quantity, without claiming a risk theorem.

Theory references use deterministic Gauss-Legendre product quadrature of orders24 and32, independent of training samples, with exact density weights. Weighted kernel eigenvalues are used only to evaluate the population operator prediction. Empirical projections use order32 quadrature. Resolution comparison is reported for every theoretical projection variance and operator linear response. This is a numerical population reference, not an exact analytical spectrum. Separately, weak-label population fits epsilon=.05/.1/.2 with bounded q=(x1+.5x2)/1.5, both ridge values and all three radii, at both quadrature orders give36 fits. Compare fitted functions with epsilon*sigma(delta)*(tau I+sigma'(delta)T)^(-1)J*q in population L2 norm. These test the general-kernel response, not a nonlinear neural SAM algorithm.

Optimization uses whitened RKHS coordinates and Newton solves with Armijo search, tolerance1e-10 and at most80 iterations. Strong ridge convexity turns a full gradient bound into a distance bound by division by tau. The omitted-gradient term assumes exact PSD arithmetic and does not certify arbitrary floating-point errors. All predeclared settings and unsuccessful optimizations remain in raw records; no selection by direction or agreement is performed. All plots are based on these raw files.
