# Data and model provenance

CIFAR-10 is attributed to Alex Krizhevsky, Vinod Nair and Geoffrey Hinton. Official description: https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~kriz/cifar.html . Downloaded byte-identical archive: https://scidata.sjtu.edu.cn/records/p4t8m-rbe26/files/cifar-10-python.tar.gz?download=1 . Official MD5: c58f30108f718f92721af3b95e74349a. Original images are not redistributed. Consult the original sources for applicable terms; no additional dataset license is asserted here.

ResNet18 and ViT-B/16 IMAGENET1K_V1 weights are supplied through torchvision. URLs and SHA256 values are in raw/*_extraction.json. Model weights are not redistributed. Selected indices, derived feature arrays, head parameters and experiment outputs are supplied for inspection. The pretrained classification heads are replaced by the identity, and the representations are not fine-tuned.
