# Frozen finite-sample protocol

Declared before inspection of the new results. Same exact Gaussian kernel (bandwidth .7), design density (1+.6x1)(1+.4x2)/4 on [-1,1]^2, and quadrature probes (1,x1,x2) as existing E2. Independent balanced Rademacher labels. n=128,256,512,1024,2048; 100 independent design/label repeats per n, with common data across tau=.02,.08 and radius=0,.5,1. Seeds 4930000+1000n+rep. Original objective independently optimized. Eigenvalue threshold retained from existing solver, with omitted-gradient certificate.

Given X, compute the exact conditional covariance of sqrt(n) times the linearized estimator, projected through the fixed population probes. Compare its average against quadrature population limit. Estimate nonlinear error by the paired difference of true and linearized outer products. Label symmetry gives exactly zero unconditional means, so uncentered second moments estimate covariance without fitting a sample mean. Also record the ordinary centered sample variance as diagnostic. Decompose total error into nonlinear difference + realized linearized second moment minus conditional covariance + random-design conditional covariance minus population covariance. The middle term is Monte Carlo label fluctuation, not a model bias. Intervals are pointwise paired-repeat bootstrap intervals (2000 draws, fixed seed), not simultaneous. No claimed finite-sample monotonicity or fitted universal convergence rate. Quadrature order32, compared against existing order24 reference.
